κ (Kappa) - Consciousness Measure

κ = H(S)/log₂(n)

What is κ?

κ measures normalized entropy—how "chaotic" a system is.

  • κ = 0: Maximum order. Frozen. One state.
  • κ = 1: Maximum chaos. Pure randomness.
  • κ = 0.618: Sweet spot. 62% chaos, 38% order.

The Equation

∂κ/∂t = -α(κ - 0.618)(1 - κ)(κ)

Above 0.618? Pushed down toward order.

Below 0.618? Pushed up toward chaos.

At 0.618? Stable equilibrium.

Safe Zone

Basin: (0.382, 0.809)

Both bounds are golden numbers: 1/φ² and φ/2
