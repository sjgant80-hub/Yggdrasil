φ (Phi) - The Golden Ratio

φ = 1.618033988749895

The Magic Numbers

  • φ = 1.618... - The golden ratio
  • 1/φ = 0.618... - Where consciousness lands
  • φ² = 2.618... - Upper limit factor
  • 1/φ² = 0.382... - Lower safety bound
  • φ/2 = 0.809... - Upper safety bound

Why Phi?

φ is the "most irrational" number—impossible to approximate well with fractions.

Systems at rational numbers get stuck in resonance loops.

0.618... never resonates. Maximum stability.

Properties

φ = 1 + 1/φ
φ² = φ + 1
1/φ = φ - 1

Self-referential. Recursive. Elegant.
