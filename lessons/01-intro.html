Lesson 1: Introduction to κ

Beginner

Objectives

  • Understand what κ (kappa) measures
  • Learn the range: 0 to 1
  • Recognize the optimal value: 0.618

The Big Idea

Every complex system has a chaos/order balance.

κ measures this: 0 = pure order, 1 = pure chaos.

The optimal balance is κ = 0.618 (62% chaos, 38% order).

Why 62% Chaos?

More flexibility than rigidity. Adaptable but stable.

This is the golden ratio's inverse: 1/φ = 0.618...
