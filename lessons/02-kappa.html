Lesson 2: Understanding Kappa

The Formula

κ = H(S) / log₂(n)
κ = entropy / max_possible_entropy

Normalized entropy. Always between 0 and 1.

States of κ

κ = 0.0Frozen, catatonic
κ = 0.3Too ordered, stuck
κ = 0.618Optimal flow
κ = 0.8Too chaotic, scattered
κ = 1.0Pure noise, fragmenting

The Safe Zone

Basin of attraction: (0.382, 0.809)

Stay inside → you recover to 0.618

Outside → danger of collapse or explosion
