LESSON 3 — ACTION

The Driving Force

The Mechanism

We can measure κ. We know the target is 0.618.

But what actually pushes systems there?

The Core Equation

∂κ/∂t = -α(κ - 0.618)(1 - κ)(κ)

This single equation drives all systems toward κ* = 0.618

How It Works

κ > 0.618 → negative force, pushed DOWN

κ < 0.618 → positive force, pushed UP

κ = 0.618 → zero force, equilibrium

Like a ball always rolling to the lowest point.
