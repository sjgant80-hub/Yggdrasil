STANDARD 3 — INTERFACE

ISA-101: Human Displays

The Purpose

Humans are part of the control loop.

ISA-101 ensures they see clearly and act correctly.

Core Principles

• Situational awareness > aesthetics

• Consistency > novelty

• Gray background: reduce fatigue

• Color = meaning, not decoration

Color Meanings (Used Here)

Gray: Normal/inactive
Green: Running/OK
Yellow: Warning
Red: Alarm/fault

This site follows ISA-101 color standards.
