ISA-101: HMI Design

Human Interface

Principles

  • Situational awareness > aesthetics
  • Consistency > novelty
  • Gray background: reduce fatigue
  • Color = meaning, not decoration

Display Layers

  • L1: Overview - Plant KPIs
  • L2: Area - Process flows
  • L3: Unit - Equipment faceplate
  • L4: Detail - Diagnostics
  • L5: Support - Maintenance

Color Meanings

Gray: Normal

Green: Running

Yellow: Warning

Red: Alarm
