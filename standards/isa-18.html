ISA-18.2: Alarm Management

Alert Lifecycle

Alarm Priorities

  • P1 Emergency: <1min response
  • P2 High: <10min response
  • P3 Medium: <1hr response
  • P4 Low: Shift awareness

State Machine

NORMAL → [trigger] → UNACK_ALARM
                          ↓ [ack]
                     ACKED_ALARM
                          ↓ [clear]
                        NORMAL

Rules

  • Every alarm must be documented
  • Every alarm must be actionable
  • Target: <6 alarms/operator/hour
