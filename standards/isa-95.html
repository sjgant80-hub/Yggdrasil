ISA-95: Enterprise Integration

Levels 0-4

The Hierarchy

  • L4: Business - ERP, BI (days-months)
  • L3: MOM - MES, LIMS (shifts-days)
  • L2: Control - SCADA, HMI (sec-hours)
  • L1: Sensing - PLC, DCS (ms-sec)
  • L0: Process - Sensors (continuous)

Key UDTs

PhysicalAsset: {id, path, parent, children}
Equipment: extends PhysicalAsset + capability
Material: {id, lot, sublots, props}
ProcessSegment: {equipment, materials, params}

Data Flows

L4→L3: Schedule, WorkOrder

L3→L4: Performance, Inventory

L3↔L2: Recipe, ProcessData
